## The challenge

Peniel Training Institute needed its programmes to reach people ready to enquire about enrolment, across more than ten professional training fields.

## What we did

Since August 2024 we have run social media strategy, content planning, multimedia production, digital campaigns and audience engagement across TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

## Results

The account reached 8.3 million Meta views, 18,700 link clicks and 32.7K page visits, plus 2.2 million TikTok views. The dean of academic affairs wrote that the work contributed to increased enrollment inquiries from audiences across Addis Ababa.
