## The challenge

Apex needed paid media and content that could be tied to business outcomes for its corporate services, training and financial offerings.

## What we did

Since August 2024 we have run full digital marketing and content production across TikTok, Facebook and Instagram: strategic planning, content development, multimedia production, social media management and paid advertising. Separately, the systems practice delivered an ERP and the Apex Tax School training platform.

## Results

In the 12 months to February 2026 the account reached 3.4 million Meta views and 2.3 million TikTok views, adding 11,200 new Meta follows. The general director wrote that paid advertising management drove measurable business outcomes and consistently generated high-quality leads.
