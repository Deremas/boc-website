## The challenge

Wow Energy Services, Smart Gas, is an LPG distributor. The operation needed a system shaped around sales, stock, agents, credit and logistics.

## What we did

We delivered a customised ERP covering sales and customer management, purchase and supplier management, inventory and multi-location stock, financial management and reporting, agents, credit approvals, logistics, role-based access and notifications, with deployment, training and post-go-live support.

## Results

The general manager wrote that the ERP enabled better visibility, improved decision-making, and streamlined operations across departments. The reference letter is dated February 2026.
