## The challenge

Lavish Medical Spa is a healthcare brand with branches in Addis Ababa, Dire Dawa and Adama. Health communication has to be accurate, culturally appropriate and approved before it is published. The Dire Dawa branch is served in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Somali.

## What we did

We run strategy, content production, on-camera hosting, community management and paid campaigns for all three branches. The Adama branch is handled by our resident Adama production team. We also built the brand’s website.

## Results

In the 12 months to February 2026, the Addis Ababa account reached 13 million TikTok views and 4.2 million Meta views. Addis Ababa TikTok also recorded 317K likes, 35K shares and 5,777 comments. Addis Ababa Facebook and Instagram recorded 59.7K interactions, 40.3K page visits and 7.5K new follows. The Dire Dawa TikTok account reached 3.9 million views, 138K likes and 15K shares.
