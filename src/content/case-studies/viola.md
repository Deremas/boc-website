## The challenge

Viola Brows & Beauty in Addis Ababa takes appointments for brows, lashes, lips and consultations. Clients needed to choose an open time and send payment proof without the back-and-forth.

## What we did

The booking site lists the active services: Brow Lamination, Nano Brows, Ombre Brows, Lash Lift, Lip Blush and Consultation. A client picks a service and a time that is still open. Booked slots, breaks, days off and times outside Monday to Saturday stay hidden. The advance is paid to one of two accounts, and the client uploads a transfer screenshot. Staff review the proof before the appointment is confirmed. After sending a request, the client can check its status.
