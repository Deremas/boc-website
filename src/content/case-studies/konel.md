## The challenge

Konel runs a 24-hour water bottling factory in three shifts. Before Blue Ocean ERP, purchasing, stock, production, quality, sales and finance were recorded on paper or in Excel.

## What we did

We implemented Blue Ocean ERP across the whole operation: purchasing, inventory with digital bin cards, integrated production and quality control, finished goods, and sales through factory, vehicle and branch channels, plus human resources and finance. We converted the factory’s manual production workbooks into system specifications and added wastage tracking by reason. Our resident team in Dire Dawa supports the plant.

## Results

The system has been in live operation since mid-2025. The general manager wrote that it brought a clear improvement in stock visibility, purchasing and sales control, production and quality control, human resources and financial management. The reference letter is dated February 2026 and was translated from Amharic.
