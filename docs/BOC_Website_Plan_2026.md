# blueoceancreatives.com — Website Plan 2026

## 1. What the Website Must Do

blueoceancreatives.com should make one thing obvious within five seconds: Blue Ocean Creatives runs the marketing in front of Ethiopian businesses and the systems behind them, and has the results to prove it.

The current site presents us as a small marketing agency, with stats counters stuck at zero and almost nothing about our ERP work, Chekela or our regional teams. The new site replaces it with the same story, numbers and evidence as the 2026 company profile.

### Goals, in order

1. **Win leads:** marketing proposal requests and ERP demo bookings.
2. **Give instant credibility** to clients and tender evaluators who check us after receiving a proposal — numbers, results, reference letters, team and legal details.
3. **Show the work:** real results, real screens, real creative.
4. **Support recruitment** as the team grows.

### Who visits and what they need

| Visitor | What they need to see | Where they go |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing manager or brand owner (health, hospitality, education, FMCG) | Results, creative quality, languages, who will manage their account | Home → Digital Marketing → Our Work → Contact |
| Factory owner, GM or finance manager | That we understand their operation, proof from similar companies, a live demo | Home → Business Systems → case studies → Book a demo |
| Tender evaluator or NGO procurement officer | Registration, experience, team, reference letters, verified results | About → Our Work → company profile download |
| Student or school | What Chekela offers and how to get the app | Chekela page → chkela.com and app stores |

### Photography and media

- Use real photos only: the team photo, studio and on-location shoots, clinic and factory visits, the office. No stock images of handshakes or robots.
- Blue Ocean ERP and client system screens shown with demo data inside clean laptop or phone frames.
- Short muted video loops (5–8 seconds) from real shoots are welcome in the hero of the Digital Marketing page, with a poster image and loaded after the page.
- Every image compressed to WebP or AVIF; the homepage should stay under about 1.5 MB.

## 5. Technical Setup

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Build tool | Cursor, following CURSOR_BUILD_BRIEF.md |
| Framework | Astro + Tailwind CSS (static, very fast on mobile networks, easy for Cursor). React only for the menu, filters, carousel and forms. |
| Content | Case studies, testimonials and clients as Markdown files, so new case studies need no code changes |
| Forms | One serverless endpoint that sends each enquiry by email and to the sales Telegram group; spam protection with Cloudflare Turnstile |
| Hosting | Cloudflare Pages (free, fast, automatic HTTPS). Keep the domain; recreate the email DNS records exactly before switching. |
| SEO | Titles and descriptions from this document, sitemap.xml, Organization and LocalBusiness structured data, Google Search Console |
| Analytics | GA4 plus Meta and TikTok pixels; track form submissions, Telegram clicks, phone clicks and profile downloads |
| Performance target | Mobile Lighthouse 90+; homepage under ~1.5 MB |
